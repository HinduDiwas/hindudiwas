HINDU DIWAS is a global campaign to unite all Hindus—irrespective of region, language, caste, or culture—to one essence of Hindu pride.

With pride to affirm Hindu identity

To connect global Hindu community

Share Hindu values & traditions

Inspire the next generation to embrace their heritage

Vision

Uniting Hindus worldwide in pride and purpose. We celebrate our shared heritage and values, building a stronger, more connected global community.

Mission

Inspire every Hindu to embrace their identity. Through events and outreach, we foster unity, cultural pride, and positive action for all.

Why Hindu Diwas?

A day to honor our roots and stand together. We affirm our traditions, share our stories, and strengthen bonds across all regions and generations.

What Next?

Join the movement on April 22 every year. Share your story, connect with others, and celebrate Hindu identity with #IamHindu—wherever you are.
